# Apple · Agentic AI Product Manager

## 岗位职责

- Build and maintain production agentic AI products serving program teams and sales teams.
- Identify high-value use cases for AI agents together with program owners and business stakeholders.
- Design, implement, and iterate on agentic workflows, including multi-agent orchestration, tool use, and structured reasoning.
- Translate ambiguous business problems into well-scoped agent products with clear success metrics.
- Own agent quality: build evaluation frameworks, monitor production accuracy, and drive prompt iteration cycles.
- Build agents end-to-end and co-develop with business teams so they can customize and operate agents after deployment.

## 任职要求

- 8+ years of technical or product experience shipping production products or features (12+ for the Senior role).
- Hands-on experience building LLM-powered applications: prompt engineering, RAG, tool use, and agent orchestration.
- Strong communication skills; the ability to translate between business needs and technical implementation.
- Experience working directly with non-technical stakeholders to define requirements.
- A track record of shipping products people actually use, with measurable impact.

## 加分项

- Experience building multi-agent systems or complex LLM orchestration pipelines.
- Familiarity with evaluation frameworks for LLM outputs (accuracy, hallucination detection, quality scoring).
- Full-stack technical skills, building end-to-end from data pipelines to user-facing interfaces.
- Experience with MCP (Model Context Protocol) or similar tool-use frameworks.
