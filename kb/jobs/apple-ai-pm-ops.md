## 岗位职责

- Define and execute product strategy, roadmap, and delivery for AI-powered products, owning the full lifecycle from concept and prototyping to launch and iteration.
- Identify high-impact AI use cases and opportunities through user research, behavioral analysis, and product analytics (retention, conversion, funnel drop-off).
- Work closely with engineering, data science, design, and business stakeholders to design user-centered systems.
- Bridge the gap between technical and non-technical teams, communicating product vision to audiences from engineers to executives.
- Build prototypes and proofs-of-concept using AI coding tools like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.

## 任职要求

- 5+ years of product management experience, with a proven track record of end-to-end product delivery and bringing products from 0 to 1.
- Experience shipping AI/ML-powered products in production environments.
- Proficiency with AI prototyping tools (Claude Code, Cursor) and AI frameworks (OpenAI APIs, LangChain, vector databases).
- Strong analytical capabilities and data-driven decision-making, including SQL and BI tools (Tableau, Power BI).
- Exceptional communication and stakeholder management; the ability to influence without direct authority.

## 加分项

- Python and SQL proficiency.
- Experience with agentic workflows and AI agent orchestration.
- Domain expertise in operations, supply chain, or enterprise enablement.
